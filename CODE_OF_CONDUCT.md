# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Context Relay a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing the observable behavior or technical claim.
- Use synthetic, redacted examples when discussing failures.
- Respect privacy boundaries and never post another person's credentials, private repository content, or personal data.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and moderation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained disruption, deliberate disclosure of private information, and knowingly unsafe demonstrations are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact options on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Security vulnerabilities must use GitHub private vulnerability reporting as described in `SECURITY.md`.

All reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled as confidentially as practical.
